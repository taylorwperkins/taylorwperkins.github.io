ABOUT

Building Geospatial Solutions That Make an Impact

I am a geospatial developer passionate about building technology that helps organizations understand, manage, and make better decisions using geographic data. My interests span GIS automation, spatial analysis, Web GIS, remote sensing, AI-assisted geospatial workflows, and software development.

Through consulting internships, university research, teaching, and independent projects, I have developed experience designing automated GIS workflows, building Python tools, creating Web GIS applications, and applying spatial analysis to real-world challenges across transportation, environmental science, infrastructure, natural resources, and public policy.

I enjoy solving complex spatial problems and am seeking opportunities where I can combine GIS, software development, automation, and data analysis to create practical solutions. My interests extend across consulting, government, utilities, transportation, engineering, environmental organizations, technology companies, research institutions, and other industries where geographic information supports decision-making.
